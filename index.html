01 · The offering

An extension of your investment team

Family offices need a lot of research but rarely have the headcount to do it. We supply a research team that adapts to your mandate, whether you need a single report or ongoing coverage.

/01

Investment Theses

We turn a theme, sector or idea into a structured, decision-ready thesis with the drivers, risks and what would change our mind.

  • Thematic & sector deep-dives
  • Bull / base / bear framing
  • Monitoring triggers
/02

Equity Research

Detailed company reports on Indian listed equities, covering the business, the financials and the valuation, written to an institutional standard.

  • Initiation-style reports
  • DCF & relative valuation
  • Earnings & event updates
/03

Live Projects

We work on specific pieces of your team's work, such as screening, diligence support, data work and market maps, with clear scopes and deadlines.

  • Pre-deal diligence support
  • Screens & market maps
  • Custom data & models
