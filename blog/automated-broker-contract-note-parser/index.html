Automated PDF Contract Note Parsing: OCR, Multi-Broker Extraction, and STP Integration

How investment desks automate broker contract note processing: OCR, multi-broker PDF parsing, statutory levy reconciliation, and Straight-Through Processing.

Short answer

Investment desks automate trade reconciliation by deploying automated PDF parsers and OCR pipelines that ingest broker contract notes across multiple institutional brokers. These engines extract executed order timestamps, net prices, ISINs, and statutory levies (STT, GST, Stamp Duty), reconciling trade fills against internal Order Management Systems (OMS) in seconds.

Key takeaways

  • Manual contract note entry is slow, prone to human error, and delays T+1 custodian trade matching.
  • Indian brokers issue contract notes in fragmented, proprietary PDF layouts with password encryption.
  • Automated parsers extract trade tables, recalculate statutory levies to ₹0.01 precision, and flag overcharges.
  • Straight-Through Processing (STP) converts unstructured PDF text into structured JSON/CSV feeds for fund accounting systems.

Every evening after market close on the NSE and BSE, Indian investment desks receive dozens of digital Broker Contract Notes. These legal documents—issued as password-protected PDF files—detail every executed trade, execution timestamp, exchange trade ID, settlement price, and statutory tax levy.

For a family office or PMS desk routing orders through three to five institutional brokers (e.g., Kotak, ICICI Securities, Axis Capital, Zerodha), processing these PDFs manually is an operational bottleneck. Junior operations personnel spend two to three hours every evening opening PDFs, manually copying execution prices and tax numbers into accounting workbooks, and checking for errors.

A single transposed digit in an executed price or an unrecorded stamp duty charge creates a Trade Break during T+1 custodian settlement.

Institutional desks replace human data entry with an Automated PDF Contract Note Parsing Pipeline.

The Structural Challenge: Multi-Broker Layout Fragmentation

Unlike mature Western markets where trades are exchanged via standardized electronic FIX drops, Indian brokers generate diverse PDF document layouts:

  • Password Protection: Every file is encrypted using combinations of the client’s PAN and date of birth.
  • Embedded Fonts and Scanned Artifacts: Many legacy institutional brokers generate PDFs with non-standard font encodings that produce garbled text when parsed using simple open-source text extractors.
  • Tax Annexure Separation: Taxes and charges are often broken into separate summary tables at the end of the document, requiring reconciliation between line-item brokerage and aggregate exchange turnover levies.

Achieving True Straight-Through Processing (STP)

By transforming unstructured PDF documents into clean, standardized JSON data objects, fund managers achieve Straight-Through Processing: - Executed fills flow directly from broker emails into the fund accounting system. - Trade breaks are identified within minutes of market close, leaving ample time to resolve discrepancies with the broker before overnight clearing cutoffs. - Operations teams are freed from manual data entry, enabling the desk to scale AUM without adding back-office headcount.

Frequently asked questions

Why are Indian broker contract notes difficult to parse automatically?

Brokers (Kotak Securities, ICICI Securities, Motilal Oswal, Zerodha) format contract notes in vastly different table layouts, font encodings, and password-protected PDF structures. Furthermore, transactions span multiple pages with separate sections for equity delivery, F&O derivatives, and statutory tax annexures.

How do automated parsers verify statutory levies?

The parser programmatically recalculates Securities Transaction Tax (0.1%), SEBI turnover charges (₹10/crore), Exchange turnover fees, Stamp Duty (0.015%), and CGST/SGST/IGST (18% on brokerage) based on gross traded value, alerting operations if the broker has overcharged the account.

Can the parser handle password-protected institutional PDFs?

Yes. Institutional parsing engines integrate automated credential vaults that programmatically decrypt files using pre-registered entity PANs or custom client passwords upon secure SFTP ingestion.
