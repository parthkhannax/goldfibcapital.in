Singapore VCC vs GIFT City Fund: Structure Comparison for Indian LPs

Comparing the Singapore Variable Capital Company (VCC) and GIFT City IFSCA Fund Management Entity (FME) for Indian family offices and fund managers.

Short answer

Singapore VCCs and GIFT City IFSCA funds are premier cross-border pooling structures for Indian capital. Singapore provides unparalleled global LP credibility, English common-law jurisprudence, and Section 13O/13U tax exemptions, while GIFT City offers direct rupee-dollar conversion clearing, zero GST, 100% corporate tax holidays, and seamless approval under the RBI's Overseas Direct Investment (ODI) framework.

Key takeaways

  • The Singapore Variable Capital Company (VCC) allows an umbrella structure where multiple sub-funds share a single corporate board while ring-fencing assets and liabilities.
  • GIFT City operates as India's offshore enclave, offering 10 consecutive years of 100% corporate tax exemptions under Section 80LA without overseas currency clearing friction.
  • International institutional LPs (US/European endowments) still strongly prefer Singapore VCCs due to MAS regulatory track record and treaty stability.
  • Indian resident promoters funding a Singapore VCC face stringent ODI round-tripping rules, making GIFT City significantly faster for deploying domestic capital.

For Indian fund managers and ultra-HNW family offices raising institutional capital, choosing the right fund domicile is a multi-million-dollar decision. Historically, managers had little choice: foreign capital was routed through Mauritius or Singapore, while domestic capital was trapped in onshore SEBI-registered vehicles.

The launch of the Variable Capital Company (VCC) by the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) in 2020 and the simultaneous emergence of GIFT City under the International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA) in India have transformed fund structuring.

Both jurisdictions offer modern, dollar-denominated fund regimes, zero capital gains tax on qualifying securities, and progressive regulatory environments. However, they serve fundamentally different LP bases and capital flows.

The Legal Innovations: Umbrella VCC vs IFSCA FME

The core structural difference lies in entity flexibility:

1. The Singapore Variable Capital Company (VCC)

Governed by the Variable Capital Companies Act 2018, the VCC is a corporate entity tailored specifically for collective investment schemes. - The Umbrella Architecture: A single VCC can operate multiple sub-funds with distinct investment strategies (e.g., Sub-Fund A for Indian Venture, Sub-Fund B for Private Debt, Sub-Fund C for Long-Short Equities). - Statutory Ring-Fencing: By law, the assets of Sub-Fund A cannot be used to discharge the liabilities of Sub-Fund B, even in the event of insolvency. - Capital Flexibility: Shares can be issued and redeemed at net asset value without shareholder approval, and dividends can be paid directly out of capital.

2. The GIFT City IFSCA Fund Regime

Regulated under the IFSCA (Fund Management) Regulations, 2022, GIFT City abolished the cumbersome SEBI AIF registration process for offshore funds, replacing it with the Fund Management Entity (FME) framework. - An FME is registered once with IFSCA and can launch multiple schemes (Cat 1, Cat 2, or Cat 3 equivalents, or Family Investment Funds) through simplified private placement memorandums (PPM). - Funds can be structured as Trusts, Companies, or Limited Liability Partnerships (LLPs).

Tax Architecture: Section 13O/13U vs Section 80LA

Tax neutrality is non-negotiable for cross-border pooling:

Singapore Tax Incentives (Section 13O and 13U)

Under the Singapore Income Tax Act, qualifying fund vehicles managed by a licensed or registered fund management company (RFMC/LFMC) can obtain tax exemption on specified income from designated investments: - Section 13O (Standard Fund): Minimum fund size of SGD 20 million at the time of application, with minimum local business spending requirements (SGD 200,000 to SGD 500,000 annually). - Section 13U (Enhanced Tier): Minimum fund size of SGD 50 million, requiring at least three investment professionals resident in Singapore.

GIFT City Tax Holiday (Section 80LA)

GIFT City provides a sovereign tax shield directly codified into the Indian Income Tax Act: - 100% Tax Exemption: 10 consecutive years out of a 15-year block under Section 80LA. - Zero Capital Gains: Complete exemption on capital gains arising from the transfer of offshore securities, debt instruments, and derivative contracts. - Zero GST: Complete exemption from Indian Goods and Services Tax (18%) on fund administration, legal, and advisory fees incurred within the IFSC.

The Decisive Domicile Matrix: How to Choose

When structuring a fund or family holding pool, managers must follow the origin of capital:

  1. If Raising Primarily from Western Institutional LPs (US Endowments, European Pension Funds): Choose Singapore. International institutional allocators have strict investment mandates that require proven regulatory regimes like MAS. GIFT City, while growing rapidly, still encounters operational friction during institutional LP diligence committees.
  2. If Deploying Domestic Indian Promoter Wealth via ODI/OPI: Choose GIFT City. Funding an offshore Singapore entity from India requires navigating stringent RBI ODI restrictions and complex round-tripping rules. GIFT City is treated as an offshore zone on Indian soil, allowing domestic corporates to invest up to 400% of net worth under the automatic route with zero foreign exchange delays.
  3. The Master-Feeder Consensus: High-scaling fund managers establish a Singapore Master Fund (to pool US and European capital) and a GIFT City Feeder Fund (to pool domestic Indian family offices), co-investing into the master strategy without tax leakage.

Frequently asked questions

What is the primary advantage of an umbrella VCC structure in Singapore?

An umbrella VCC allows a fund manager to launch multiple sub-funds (e.g., a private credit fund, a venture fund, and a public equity fund) under a single legal entity. Assets and liabilities of each sub-fund are legally segregated by statute, significantly lowering administrative and regulatory filing costs.

Can Indian resident promoters set up a Singapore VCC using the ODI route?

Yes, but it requires compliance with the Foreign Exchange Management (Overseas Investment) Rules, 2022. Investments in foreign financial services entities require prior regulatory clearances and must satisfy strict substance criteria to avoid being classified as illegal round-tripping.

How does taxation compare between Singapore VCC and GIFT City AIFs?

Singapore VCCs utilize Section 13O or 13U tax incentive schemes to achieve complete exemption on specified investment income. GIFT City AIFs enjoy a statutory 10-year 100% corporate tax holiday under Section 80LA, zero GST on fund management fees, and zero capital gains on eligible securities.
