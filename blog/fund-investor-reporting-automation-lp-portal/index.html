Automating LP Reporting for Indian AIFs: Factsheets, Capital Accounts, and IRR Engines

How Indian AIF fund managers automate monthly LP reporting: factsheet generation, Capital Account Statements (CAS), and personalized IRR calculations.

Short answer

Indian AIF managers automate LP reporting by running digital pipelines that ingest custodian data, generate branded monthly performance factsheets, compute personalized tranche-level IRRs, reconcile unfunded capital commitments, and auto-dispatch digital Capital Account Statements (CAS) with encrypted one-click access for institutional and family office investors.

Key takeaways

  • Manual generation of monthly factsheets and Capital Account Statements takes 10 to 15 business days following month-end.
  • Family office LPs demand real-time transparency: tranche-level XIRRs, hurdle tracking, and underlying portfolio look-throughs.
  • Automated PDF engines dynamically generate pixel-perfect branded investor reports directly from reconciled accounting databases.
  • SEBI regulations require quarterly and annual portfolio disclosures formatted to standardized regulatory reporting templates.

In Indian fund management, alpha generation is only half the battle. The other half is Investor Relations and LP Reporting.

For Category 2 and Category 3 Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs), the first two weeks of every month are an operational scramble. Operations teams manually extract trial balances, format Excel spreadsheets, copy portfolio performance metrics into PowerPoint factsheets, and draft individual Capital Account Statements (CAS) for dozens of family office LPs.

This manual process is slow, vulnerable to embarrassing typographical errors, and results in delayed reports that frustrate institutional allocators.

Leading Indian fund managers are replacing manual reporting with Automated LP Reporting Engines.

The Three Components of Institutional LP Reporting

An automated reporting engine delivers three standardized outputs:

1. The Dynamic Fund Factsheet

Factsheet generation is automated using headless browser rendering (Puppeteer/Playwright) paired with HTML/CSS templates. The engine pulls reconciled month-end data, computes benchmark tracking error, renders high-resolution SVG charts, and compiles a publication-ready PDF in seconds.

2. Personalized Tranche-Level XIRR

Family office CIOs despise generic fund-level marketing numbers. If an LP entered in the Third Close in November, their personal return is distinct from an anchor investor who funded in January. The engine computes Investor-Specific XIRR: $$\sum_{t=0}^{T} \frac{C_t}{(1 + \text{XIRR})^{t / 365}} = 0$$ Where $C_t$ represents the investor’s exact cash calls (negative) and cash distributions (positive), culminating in current net asset value.

Frequently asked questions

What is included in an AIF Capital Account Statement (CAS)?

A CAS details the individual LP's opening capital balance, capital drawdowns called during the period, distributions paid out (separated into return of capital vs profit gains), management fee and expense deductions, unrealized ending NAV balance, and net since-inception IRR.

How does tranche-level IRR differ from fund-level IRR?

In private equity and venture funds, LPs enter the fund at different closes and pay capital calls on varying dates. Tranche-level IRR calculates the exact time-weighted and money-weighted return (XIRR) based on the specific investor's actual cash-in and cash-out dates, rather than the fund's aggregate benchmark.

Can automated LP reporting support multi-currency views for offshore investors?

Yes. Modern reporting engines ingest daily RBI reference exchange rates, generating parallel investor views in both Indian Rupees (INR) and US Dollars (USD) for international LPs participating via GIFT City or FPI routes.
