Students who want to work in equity research usually ask what to learn. The better question is: **what can I do on day one that saves a senior analyst time?** Answer that and you're useful from the start, not after six months of training.

The top three are unglamorous, and that's the point. A reviewer can teach valuation nuance in a conversation. They can't afford to recheck every number in a model.

## A 12-week roadmap

```viz
type: timeline
title: "From zero to a reviewed initiation in 12 weeks"
events:
  - when: "Weeks 1–2"
    title: "Read like an analyst"
    sub: "Take one listed company. Read three years of annual reports in buy-side order: auditor, notes, cash flows, then the narrative."
  - when: "Weeks 3–5"
    title: "Build the model"
    sub: "Three statements, driver-based revenue by segment, history that reconciles to filings within rounding."
    hl: true
  - when: "Weeks 6–7"
    title: "Value it"
    sub: "DCF with a sensitivity grid, peer multiples, and a bear/base/bull range with named drivers."
  - when: "Week 8"
    title: "Find the variant view"
    sub: "Read eight quarters of concall transcripts. Where do you disagree with consensus, and what would prove you wrong?"
  - when: "Weeks 9–10"
    title: "Write it"
    sub: "One-page summary first, then supporting sections. Every number footnoted."
    hl: true
  - when: "Weeks 11–12"
    title: "Get it reviewed and automate one thing"
    sub: "Have an experienced person tear it apart. Then script one repetitive task, such as pulling quarterly results into the model."
caption: "One company, done properly, teaches more than ten done superficially. The guides linked below cover each stage."
```

## Certifications vs portfolio

Certifications such as the CFA programme give a strong, structured foundation and signal commitment. But anyone hiring for research will ask one question: **show me something you've written.** A portfolio of two or three reviewed initiations on real Indian companies, with models, is the strongest signal a student can send.

## The automation edge

Analysts who can automate their own data work now have a real advantage. Pulling results into a model, running a screen or tracking shareholding changes with a short script frees hours for analysis every week. The skills overlap with what we describe in [AI for investment research](/blog/ai-investment-research-automation-india/) and [stock screening for Indian markets](/blog/stock-screening-india-factor-models/).
