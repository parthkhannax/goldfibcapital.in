About Goldfib Capital

Goldfib Capital is the outsourced research intelligence and financial engineering desk for family offices and portfolio managers across India, Singapore, and the UAE. We eliminate the multi-crore overhead of a bloated in-house analyst bullpen while delivering institutional-grade deal diligence, shadow NAV reconciliation, and quantitative surveillance.

Why Goldfib Exists

Traditional equity research is structurally broken for family offices. Bulge-bracket sell-side notes are designed to generate brokerage turnover, often clouded by investment banking conflicts and backward-looking price targets. Meanwhile, building an internal team of senior equity analysts, forensic accountants, and data engineers costs upwards of ₹1.5 Cr annually in salary alone, with painful turnover and uneven output quality.

Goldfib Capital solves this coverage gap with a high-throughput, conflict-free model. We pair rigorous buy-side financial modeling with automated extraction pipelines across BSE/NSE filings, MCA disclosures, and cross-border regulatory registries.
